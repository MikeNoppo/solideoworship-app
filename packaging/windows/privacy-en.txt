SOLIDEOWORSHIP PRIVACY POLICY & LICENSE
=======================================

SolideoWorship is built to serve churches, never to harvest data.

1. WORSHIP DATA & LOCAL FILES STAY ON YOUR COMPUTER
   SolideoWorship never reads, copies, or transmits any file outside the application. All of your songs, lyrics, Bibles, and schedules remain entirely on your own computer.

2. ANONYMOUS USAGE STATISTICS (OPTIONAL)
   By default, the application sends an anonymous usage ping at most once per day (containing a random installation ID, app version, operating system, CPU architecture, and interface language code) solely so we know which platforms and versions are in active use.
   You can turn this off at any time in:
   Settings -> General -> Send anonymous usage statistics.

3. CRASH & ISSUE REPORTS (WITH YOUR CONSENT)
   If the application stops unexpectedly, technical error details are saved locally first and are never sent behind your back. On the next launch, you can review, send, or discard the report.
